FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Let's hop on a call.

Will this bring me new leads?

No, and that's on purpose. Your leads come from referrals, search, ads and word of mouth. Our job is to make sure that when they look you up, they like what they find and feel ready to reach out.

What if I'm not comfortable on camera?

Most clients aren't at first. We ask the questions, you answer the way you would with a customer, and we edit out the pauses. You see the footage as we go.

How much of my time does it take?

About two hours every six months when you post once a week: a 20-minute planning call, around 90 minutes of filming and a few minutes to approve. Posting more often adds some filming time.

Do you post for us?

Yes. We write captions, schedule and post to one Facebook Page and one Instagram account. You keep ownership of your accounts and give us access, never your password.

What if I don't like a video?

You approve every step: the plan, each shot while we film, and the finished batch, with a round of edits included. By the time anything posts, you've already said yes to it.

What's the commitment?

Plans run for 6 or 12 months. Pay in full or in monthly payments. 12-month plans save 10%. We never renew automatically.

Do you work outside Salt Lake City?

Yes, anywhere in the US. Travel is included on every plan except once a week for 6 months.
