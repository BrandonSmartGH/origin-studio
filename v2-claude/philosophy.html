Our philosophy

Social media doesn't find your customers.

It convinces them. Here's how we think about social video for businesses built on trust.

The part of the sale you never see.

Every lead who reaches out to you did something first. They looked you up. A friend mentioned your name, or they found you on Google, or they saw your truck. Then, before they called, they opened your Instagram or your Facebook page.

You'll never see that moment. But it decides whether they call you or keep looking. We call it the trust gap.

An empty page, or a last post from two years ago, creates doubt at exactly the wrong time. A steady feed of you answering questions, showing your work and introducing your people does the opposite. It closes the gap before you ever say hello.

What they're really asking.

Nobody scrolls your page thinking about content strategy. They're quietly asking three questions. Every video we make answers one of them.

Views are the wrong scoreboard.

Chasing viral reach brings in strangers from other states. For a dentist in Sandy or a builder in Bend, a thousand views from the wrong people are worth less than ten from the right ones.

The audience that matters is small and specific: the people who heard your name this month and are deciding whether to call. We make videos for them.

The goal isn't to be seen by everyone.
It's to be trusted by the people already looking.

Familiarity adds up.

Every video is a small meeting. The more of them a lead has had, the more your first real conversation feels like your second. That's why how often you post matters.

What we don't do.

We don't run ads, chase trends or promise followers. If you want social media to bring in new leads on its own, an ad agency is a better fit, and we'll happily say so.

What we do is make every lead you already get more likely to say yes. That includes the leads your ads bring in.

Why we do it for you.

Consistency is the whole game, and it's the first thing to slip when you're busy running a business. So we take it off your plate. One planning call, one filming session, and months of videos, with your approval at every step.
